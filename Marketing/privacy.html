postcardfilm.

privacy policy · last updated 29 august 2026

postcardfilm is a small instant-camera app for iphone. this page explains what the app does with your photos and settings.

short version: we don’t collect your data. prints stay on your phone.

postcardfilm does not:

  • create accounts or ask you to sign in
  • upload photos to a server or the cloud
  • use analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • sell or share personal information

what stays on your device:

  • camera — used only to take prints
  • photos library (add-only) — used only when you choose to save a print; the app does not read your existing library
  • prints & captions — stored in the app’s local documents on your iphone
  • settings — caption preferences saved locally with userdefaults

if you delete the app, local prints and settings on that device are removed with it (unless you already saved copies to photos).

postcardfilm is not affiliated with polaroid or fujifilm.
